THE FINAL WHISTLE

How to play & arcade rules

Batting

Easy controls are the default: tap HIT or press Space while the indicator is green. The game aims and selects a shot for you. Better timing scores more. In Advanced controls: Use A/D or tap a lane to meet the ball. Choose defence (safe singles), drive (up to four), or loft (six with excellent timing). Swing with Space or the button when the ball reaches the highlighted line.

Bowling

In Easy controls, simply press Bowl for an automatically chosen delivery. Advanced controls let you choose off, stumps, or leg and vary pace, slower balls, and spin. The batter cannot see your selection until you bowl. Aim at the stumps to punish a missed shot.

The chase

Six legal balls make an over. Each player controls an innings; chase the first score plus one. Equal scores are a tie. This is arcade cricket: automatic fielding, no extras, LBW, run-outs, or individual batsman rotation.

Superpowers

Each avatar has a fixed batting and bowling power. Choose your personality in the lobby. One charge per role per over; no stacking or extra base stats. Easy controls activate powers automatically; Advanced lets you choose when. Powers refresh each over and innings. Six remains the maximum per ball. Read the avatar cards for strengths and limits. Classic disables all powers.

Connection

Use separate devices or browser profiles for two players. A short run-up gives both clients time to see each delivery. Stay on the game tab while batting. If disconnected, bowling deadlines and missed shots still count.
